# doinstruct

> doinstruct is an AI-powered training platform that helps companies "make operational excellence part of daily work." It turns internal knowledge and expertise into mobile-first training for frontline and deskless teams, delivered in 35+ languages to improve quality, safety, and compliance across every shift.

doinstruct was founded in Osnabrück, Germany (2021) and is now headquartered in Brooklyn, NY, with an office in Berlin. Its platform serves 400+ frontline teams across food & beverage, logistics, manufacturing, construction, and general compliance, with 400+ ready-made lessons. The site is bilingual — English pages are listed below and their German equivalents live under the `/de/` prefix (e.g. `https://www.doinstruct.com/de/product/genius`).

## Products
- [Genius](https://www.doinstruct.com/product/genius): AI-powered training creation and translations — turns documents and expert knowledge into ready-to-use courses in minutes.
- [Training Library](https://www.doinstruct.com/product/training-library): Ready-made training content for frontline operations, with 400+ lessons on safety, hygiene, and compliance.
- [Manager Experience](https://www.doinstruct.com/product/manager): The control center for training and compliance — assign, track, and document training across teams.
- [Learner Experience](https://www.doinstruct.com/product/learner): Mobile-first training every frontline employee completes in their own language.

- [Food & Beverage](https://www.doinstruct.com/solutions/food-and-beverage): Food-safety, hygiene, and HACCP training for food producers and processors.
